## User Research — Six Participants

Six anonymized first-time SIP investor responses were collected asynchronously using a structured Google Form on October 28–29, 2026.

[View six-participant research notes](Groww_Form_Based_Research_Notes.md)

Methodology: Structured form-based qualitative research, not live moderated interviews. Findings are directional and inform the SIP Continuity Copilot's payment, expense, and market-uncertainty scenarios.
